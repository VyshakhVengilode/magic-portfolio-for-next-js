## "നാരി" (Naari)
This cover art for the college magazine **"നാരി" (Naari)** of **Govt. ITI for Women**, Kannur, is a powerful and symbolic piece focused on **women's empowerment**, **social justice**, and **constitutional values**.

![Naari](/images/projects/project-01/naari-full.jpg)

## Visual Concept

**1. Front Cover:**
    - A determined young woman dressed in traditional attire holds **The Constitution of India** close to her chest.
    - The book cover mentions "**Liberty**, **Equality**, **Fraternity**", echoing the **Preamble**.
    - **Backdrop**: A busy urban street filled with people, indicating her presence and resistance in public spaces often dominated by men.
    - Her gaze is direct, showing resolve and courage.

**2. Back Cover:**
    - A fist inside the **♀** (female gender symbol) conveys feminist resistance and strength.
    - **Background**: A crowd of diverse individuals in a muted red tone—representing unity and collective struggle.
    - **Top text**: **സമത്വം | സാഹോദര്യം | സ്വാതന്ത്ര്യം (Equality | Fraternity | Freedom)**.

## Key Themes:

**1. Empowerment through Knowledge:**
    - The Constitution symbolizes legal literacy and awareness of rights.

**2. Feminism and Social Change:**
    - The gender symbol + raised fist is a universal emblem of women's movements.
    - The woman is not just a passive figure—she's a participant in public life.

**3. Civic Awareness & Democracy:**
    - The values shown on the book and in the back cover text connect to India's constitutional ethos.

**4. Visual Contrast & Symbolism:**
    - The use of monochrome illustration on a bold red background grabs attention and evokes urgency, strength, and determination.
