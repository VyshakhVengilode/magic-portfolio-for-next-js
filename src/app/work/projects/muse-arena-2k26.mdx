## MUSE ARENA 2K26 | Kerala's Biggest Fashion & Bridal Show

The visual branding for **MUSE ARENA 2K26**, a major fashion and bridal event organised by **Global Institute of Textile Designing (GITD)**, one of Kerala's leading fashion designing institutions with over 100 branches and franchises across the state.

Scheduled for **27 November 2026 at Dinesh Auditorium, Kannur**, the event brings together emerging fashion talent from across Kerala, creating a platform for creativity, style, and artistic expression.

With more than **100 selected models**, **2,000+ designers**, and an anticipated audience of **5,000+ attendees**, MUSE ARENA 2K26 is positioned as one of Kerala's notable intercollegiate fashion and bridal contests.

My contribution was the **branding and visual identity development** for this mega event, creating a cohesive visual language that reflects its scale, energy, glamour, and fashion-forward character.

## Branding Concept

**1. Event Identity:**

- Developed the visual branding for MUSE ARENA 2K26, establishing a distinctive identity for the event.
- Focused on communicating the glamour, creativity, and competitive spirit of fashion and bridal design.
- Created a visual direction suitable for a large-scale fashion event with participation from across Kerala.

**2. Visual Communication:**

- Maintained a consistent branding approach across event promotional materials.
- Focused on strong visual hierarchy, typography, and impactful presentation.
- Designed communication that captures attention while maintaining a recognisable event identity.

**3. Event Positioning:**

- Reflected GITD's presence as a leading fashion designing institution in Kerala.
- Positioned MUSE ARENA 2K26 as a platform celebrating emerging models, designers, and creative talent.
- Emphasised the event's scale, professional character, and statewide participation.
