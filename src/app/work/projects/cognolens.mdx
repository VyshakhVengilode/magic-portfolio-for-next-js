## "നാരി" (Naari)
Developed a rule-based **AI system** for detecting and restructuring **cognitively biased statements**, grounded in **cognitive psychology** and **epistemology**. Emphasized **methodological rigor**, **uncertainty preservation**, and **non-persuasive reasoning**.

![Cognolens](/images/projects/project-01/cl-2.jpg)

## Core Concepts:

**1. Epistemic Clarity through Science:**
- CognoLens applies principles from cognitive psychology and epistemology to analyze how statements are formed and justified.

**2. Cognitive Bias Analysis:**
- The system identifies structured reasoning errors such as overgeneralization, confirmation bias, and false causality.

**3. Rule-Governed Reasoning System:**
- Designed as a constrained analytical tool, CognoLens follows defined rules rather than persuasive or opinion-driven generation.

**4. Responsible Use of AI:**
- Positions artificial intelligence as a tool for reducing cognitive distortion and supporting clear, evidence-based thought.
